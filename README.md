One sharp, funny loading-screen tip for stressed developers and AI coding agents, in 200 characters or fewer.

Competition: [Loading Screen Gold: A Tip in 200 Characters](https://make.fun/c/u-ricoxyz-loading-screen-gold-a-tip-in-200-characters-2b1df270)

## Entry

> Your agent just refactored your code, your tests, and your confidence. Breathe: you're the only dependency it can't reinstall.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single-line tip to `/output/entry.txt` (pass an output path as an argument for local testing). It validates the entry first: one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.
